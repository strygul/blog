How should the tetsubin post look?

Choose the balance between historical clarity and the tactile pleasure of the objects. The final post will use properly credited, permission-safe images; these source-page photographs are only standing in for the layout.

A · Museum catalogue

Objects first

Large, quiet kettle portraits with detailed captions. Elegant and scholarly, but the making traditions may feel abstract.

B · Workshop documentary

Hands and process

Moulds, casting, chiselling, handles, and the people doing the work. Lively and intimate, but weaker for comparing finished regional styles.

C · Recommended

Regional visual essay

Each tradition gets a finished kettle, a making image, and a close detail. A small map and comparison plate make the differences legible at a glance.

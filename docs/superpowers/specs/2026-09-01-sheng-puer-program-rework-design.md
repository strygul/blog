# Sheng Puer Comparative Program Rework Design

**Date:** 2026-09-01

**Status:** Approved in conversation

**Scope:** Replace the 24-flight curriculum with a smaller, validity-first program and keep its research, purchasing data, public introduction, and tests consistent.

## Problem

The existing curriculum contains useful teas and research, but it labels too many exercises as sensory flights. Several comparisons change many variables at once or ask the drinker to infer concepts that do not have a unique taste, including vendor philosophy, prestige, provenance, tree age, intended aging, and production model. The 12-core-plus-12-advanced structure also rewards filling a quota and creates redundant purchases.

The rework must preserve the strongest comparisons, convert market and evidence questions into explicit methods labs, and remove weak standalone modules. Quality of inference takes priority over module count.

## Goals

- Publish no more than 12 genuine sensory flights, divided into six foundations and six advanced comparisons.
- Add three methods labs for evidence and expectation questions that cannot be established from flavor alone.
- Give every sensory flight a repeatable comparison procedure and an explicit ceiling on its conclusions.
- Reuse teas deliberately and offer a coherent foundation basket and complete-program basket.
- Recheck selected offers, sample sizes, availability, and prices for a September 2026 snapshot.
- Keep factual observations, vendor claims, practitioner hypotheses, and the author's inferences visibly distinct.
- Keep the detailed research guide, catalog, notebook, public introduction, and automated tests internally consistent.

## Non-goals

- Proving universal regional flavor profiles from one pair of commercial teas.
- Authenticating origin, tree age, recipe, storage, or production claims by taste.
- Ranking vendors or treating omission as a judgment of quality.
- Treating price as a sensory attribute or expensive tea as inherently better.
- Constructing laboratory-grade causal experiments from incomplete commercial records.
- Preserving 24 modules, three price tiers, or any existing comparison solely for continuity.

## Quality Gate

Twelve sensory flights are a maximum and the expected result, not a quota. A flight remains in the published program only if the September 2026 audit finds a purchasable pair or trio that makes the stated comparison educationally credible. If no candidate meets the flight's matching and documentation requirements, remove that flight and recalculate the sequence and baskets. Do not fill the gap with a weak comparison.

Each retained sensory flight must satisfy all of the following:

1. The teas are likely to produce a perceptible comparison under a shared brewing protocol.
2. The question concerns sensory differences between the selected teas, not authentication or an abstract market claim.
3. The principal variable is matched as closely as current commercial samples allow.
4. Remaining confounders are named before the tasting interpretation.
5. The allowed conclusion is limited to the selected teas unless stronger evidence exists.
6. The samples are currently purchasable in practical quantities or have a qualified substitute.

## Curriculum Architecture

The program is one sequence. The first six sensory flights establish broad coordinates; the next six deepen earlier comparisons. Methods labs are interleaved where their tools become useful.

| Order | Type | Module | Purpose |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Foundation flight | Development states within Dayi 7542 | Compare three development points while treating age, batch, processing, and storage as intertwined rather than attributing all differences to age. |
| 2 | Foundation flight | One tea, two storage histories | Use the closest available matched production to make the program's strongest storage comparison. |
| 3 | Methods lab | Storage evidence audit | Distinguish what a matched pair, storage sampler, city label, and vendor warehouse description can each support. |
| 4 | Foundation flight | Yiwu and Bulang | Establish a broad contrast using closely matched commercial examples without turning tendencies into terroir laws. |
| 5 | Foundation flight | Xishuangbanna and Lincang | Establish a second broad regional contrast under the same inference limits. |
| 6 | Foundation flight | Dayi 7542 and 8582 | Compare two recipes from one factory and a closely matched period. |
| 7 | Foundation flight | Dayi and Xiaguan | Compare selected factory expressions while acknowledging recipe, processing, compression, and storage confounders. |
| 8 | Methods lab | Claims are not flavors | Audit production-model, provenance, tree-age, single-origin, processing, compression, disclosure, and aging claims without treating them as directly tastable facts. |
| 9 | Advanced flight | Yiwu within Yiwu | Compare two well-documented subregional examples after the broad Yiwu contrast. |
| 10 | Advanced flight | Lao Man'e bitterness spectrum | Compare credible Lao Man'e examples with meaningfully different bitterness structures without using taste to authenticate varietal or tree-age claims. |
| 11 | Advanced flight | Lincang within Lincang | Compare Mengku and Bangdong examples after the broader regional flight. |
| 12 | Advanced flight | Spring and autumn from one origin | Retain only if seller, producer, origin, year, processing, and format are closely matched enough for season to be a plausible focus. |

| 14 | Advanced flight | Two traditional Hong Kong storage profiles | Compare complete stored expressions; do not attribute differences to an undocumented airing period or a single storage-intensity variable. |
| 15 | Methods lab | Blind value and expectation | Compare blind and informed judgments to study expectation effects, not a supposed taste of fame or price. |

The labels "foundation" and "advanced" describe learning order, not tea quality or price.

## Shared Sensory Protocol

Every flight compares two or three teas. The detailed guide will provide one shared brewing protocol rather than repeat incomplete instructions in every module:

- Use identical 100 ml neutral porcelain vessels, 5 g of leaf per vessel, one water source, water at 100 °C, one five-second rinse, and infusions of 10, 10, 15, 20, 30, and 45 seconds. Continue beyond six infusions when the teas remain expressive.
- Randomize samples under neutral codes and complete the first notes and preference ranking before revealing their identities.
- Record dry and wet-leaf aroma, liquor aroma, bitterness, sweetness, astringency, texture, aftertaste, perceived development, and evolution across infusions.
- Repeat the comparison on a different day with new codes.
- Treat a principal contrast as useful only when it is reasonably repeatable. Report an unstable or unclear result as inconclusive.
- Permit practical pacing accommodations, but disclose any parameter changed for only one tea because that ends the controlled side-by-side phase.

The same defaults apply across flights. Any module-specific exception must state why it is needed and how it limits comparison. Vessel capacity means the practical brewing volume used consistently across the set, not merely the manufacturer's nominal maximum.

## Required Flight Template

Every sensory flight must include these fields in this order:

1. **Question** — one testable sensory question.
2. **Difficulty and path** — foundation or advanced, with a short reason.
3. **Suggested teas** — full offer identity, portion, price, vendor, and claim status.
4. **Why this set** — matching logic and educational value.
5. **Hold constant** — practical controls for the session.
6. **Observe** — neutral sensory axes, never a required answer.
7. **Confounders** — variables the purchase cannot control.
8. **Allowed conclusion** — the strongest defensible interpretation.
9. **Cannot establish** — prohibited causal, regional, or authentication claims.
10. **Substitution rule** — properties a replacement must preserve.
11. **Cost and reuse** — incremental purchase cost and previously owned teas.

Regional conclusions must refer to the selected examples. Recipe and factory comparisons must not assign differences to recipe or producer alone when batch, material, processing, compression, or storage remain unmatched. Storage language must distinguish a matched production from unrelated teas carrying city or warehouse labels.

## Methods Labs

Methods labs are visibly separated from sensory flights in headings, numbering, prose, and tests.

### Storage Evidence Audit

This lab reuses the matched storage pair and documentation already collected for selected storage examples. It sorts evidence into four levels: matched commercial pair, multi-tea sampler, city or climate label, and seller storage narrative. The output is a table of supported conclusions and unsupported inferences. It adds no required purchase.

### Claims Are Not Flavors

This lab reuses selected product pages and teas to classify claims about factory versus boutique production, blend construction, single-area origin, tree age, compression, processing, intended aging, and disclosure philosophy. Participants may compare the teas, but the result concerns the relationship between documentation and experience; it does not authenticate the claims. It adds no required purchase.

### Blind Value and Expectation

This lab uses a famous-name and a less-famous comparison selected as closely as practical in year, broad region, seller, sample format, and style. Participants record blind sensory scores, preference, and estimated value, then reveal names and prices and repeat the evaluation. The measured result is any change in the participant's judgment after disclosure. The lab must not claim that price, prestige, scarcity, or authenticity was tasted. The implementation may retain the current Bingdao and Mo Lie candidates only if September revalidation confirms that they remain the strongest practical pair.

## Vendor Research and Purchasing Model

TeaDB remains a curriculum and vendor-discovery source, not proof of current inventory. Current purchasing facts must come from vendor pages or public storefront data checked in September 2026.

The research will retain the investigated pool of established and community-recommended vendors serving Western buyers. A vendor or offer enters the recommendation only when it contributes to a strong comparison. Selection criteria, in order, are:

1. validity of the comparison;
2. documentation of identity, recipe or origin, batch, harvest, processing, and storage where relevant;
3. availability of practical sample sizes;
4. reuse across modules;
5. total delivered purchasing burden, including order consolidation concerns;
6. price per 10 g and total price.

Reputation alone is not a selection criterion. The public article must say that the survey is neither a comprehensive vendor guide nor an endorsement, that omissions are not quality judgments, and that product claims remain vendor claims unless independently documented.

The purchasing presentation has two cumulative paths:

- **Foundation basket:** all unique offers required for the six foundation flights. The first two methods labs reuse its teas and documentation.
- **Complete-program basket:** the union of the foundation basket, six advanced flights, and any samples required only for the blind value and expectation lab.

There are no cheaper substitutions that weaken the lesson and no prestige upgrades presented as higher quality. Qualified reserves are listed only when they preserve or improve the module's controls. Each offer is counted once in cumulative totals, regardless of reuse.

For every selected listing, record the native price and currency, purchasable quantity, EUR conversion, EUR per 10 g, vendor location, checked date, availability status, module roles, and whether each material or storage statement is observed, vendor-claimed, inferred, or unknown. Shipping, tax, card spread, and import costs remain excluded but must be disclosed.

The public introduction should explain the method and roadmap without reproducing the full guide. It must preserve the approved grey preface and the uncropped hero image already added to the post.

## Data Flow and Failure Handling

1. Revalidate candidate product pages and storefront data.
2. Record raw offer facts and evidence status in the catalog and notebook.
3. Select only candidates that pass the quality gate.
4. Assign each selected offer to module roles.
5. Derive unique foundation and complete-program baskets from those roles.
6. Calculate normalized and cumulative prices without double-counting.
7. Write the detailed guide from the verified selections.
8. Publish only the roadmap, methodology, and derived headline totals in the introduction.
9. Assert the published structure and totals in tests.

If a listing is unavailable, ambiguous, or materially changed, first apply the module's substitution rule. If no qualified substitute exists, remove the module or mark it as a future exercise outside the purchasable program. Do not silently use cached pricing, broaden a claim, or introduce a poorly matched tea to preserve the count.

## Verification

Automated checks must cover:

- no more than 12 sensory flights and exactly three clearly labelled methods labs;
- roadmap order and foundation/advanced classification;
- absence of removed standalone flight titles and obsolete 24-flight language;
- presence of blind-first, reveal, repeat-session, and inconclusive-result guidance;
- explicit allowed-conclusion and cannot-establish language in every detailed flight;
- reuse and unique-offer arithmetic for both baskets;
- agreement between catalog calculations, detailed guide totals, and public totals;
- September 2026 price and availability wording;
- investigated vendor pool, selection criteria, non-endorsement, and vendor-claim caveats;
- preservation of the grey preface and full uncropped hero image.

The project build and relevant test suite must pass after the rewrite. A manual rendered-page review must confirm that the revised roadmap is readable and that the hero remains fully visible at desktop and narrow viewport widths.

## Acceptance Criteria

The rework is complete when:

- every published sensory flight passes the quality gate and follows the shared template;
- methods labs are not described or numbered as sensory flights;
- all current offers and prices are backed by a September 2026 check;
- the two baskets contain only required, non-duplicated purchases and their totals reconcile across artifacts;
- rejected and removed comparisons do not survive as public recommendations;
- the public article accurately summarizes the detailed research without overstating what can be tasted;
- automated tests, the production build, and manual page inspection pass.

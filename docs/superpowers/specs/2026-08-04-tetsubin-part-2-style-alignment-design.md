# Tetsubin Part 2 Style Alignment

## Goal

Rewrite `src/content/tea/tetsubin-history-2-morioka.md` so it reads as the natural continuation of Part 1: clear, narrative, approachable, and grounded in sources.

## Approach

Use a narrative rebuild rather than a surface copy edit. Preserve the article's strongest facts, links, credits, and useful images, while removing repeated caveats, dossier-style evidence boxes, and tables that interrupt the story.

The article will:

- use `##` headings and the same explanatory, first-person voice as Part 1;
- open with a question or tension that carries the reader from the general history into Morioka;
- explain the regional history and casting method before introducing the workshops;
- give Suzuki Morihisa, Kunzan, Iwachu, and Kamasada distinct, memorable profiles;
- state uncertainty once, where it matters, in plain language;
- turn identification research into concise practical guidance rather than repeated matrices;
- close by explaining what the four workshops reveal about the Nambu name and lead naturally into Part 3.

## Content Boundaries

No new historical claims are needed. Existing sources remain the evidence base. Dense material may be compressed or removed when it repeats a point, but factual distinctions—especially conflicting dates, uncertain marks, and differences in production—must remain accurate.

The frontmatter, image paths, licences, source links, previous/next navigation, and accessible image markup must remain valid. Unused diagrams may stay in the repository; deleting assets is outside this edit.

## Verification

Run the project build to catch Markdown, HTML, and link-path rendering errors. Review the final Markdown for consistent heading hierarchy, readable paragraph length, preserved credits, and a clear narrative progression matching Part 1.

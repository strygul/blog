# Tetsubin Part 1 Voice Rewrite

## Goal

Rewrite the existing article as a lively investigation in the author's established tea-blog voice. The story begins with HOJO's compelling account, follows the dates that do not quite fit, and lets each section resolve another part of the historical puzzle.

## Voice

- Use first person only for the real research journey: reading, doubting, comparing, and discovering.
- Address the reader directly where it makes the history easier to follow.
- Prefer short paragraphs, concrete images, sharp transitions, and occasional punch-line verdicts.
- Preserve uncertainty without repeating academic disclaimers.
- Do not invent ownership, travel, conversations, handling, tasting, or other firsthand tetsubin experience.

## Scope

Keep the article's facts, sources, images, captions, diagrams, title-case section headings, and overall chronology. Rewrite the introduction, exposition, transitions, and ending. Trim repetition where the same qualification is already established. End with a clear invitation into the five regional articles that follow.

## Acceptance

The revised post reads as a story of a historical misconception being investigated, remains source-faithful, and builds successfully without layout or asset changes.

# Tetsubin history series — sources ledger

**Access date:** 2026-07-31

## Evidence rules

- Paraphrase evidence; do not copy long passages into this ledger or the posts.
- Record disagreements as separate rows, with the competing source and the unresolved point stated plainly.
- Identify sources as **institutional**, **government/association**, **curator/research**, **workshop**, **retailer**, or **auction**. Retailer and auction material may supply discovery, comparison, or object context, but not the sole support for a historical date, lineage, or attribution.
- Prefer museum records for objects, dates, marks, materials, and provenance; prefer government/association and curator/research material for regional history; attribute workshop claims to the workshop.
- `Confidence` means the strength of the stated evidence, not a judgement about a maker or region. Do not promote a conflict to a precise claim without stronger documentation.
- **Maker-mark audit requirement:** for every mark claim, record the technique (cast, impressed, engraved, chiseled, lid inscription, box inscription, label, or certificate), location, original characters, normalized reading, romanization and English meaning where meaningful, documented date or generation range (or `range not established`), a complete-kettle context image or an explicit evidentiary limit, source, and confidence (`documented`, `strongly attributed`, or `uncertain`).
- **Maker-mark warning requirement:** say that lids, handles, boxes, and certificates can be replacements or later pairings; a signed lid does not authenticate the body; historical marks can be copied; workshops also made unmarked work; and regional style alone cannot prove a maker or origin.

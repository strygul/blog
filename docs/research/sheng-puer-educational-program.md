# Learning sheng puer through comparative flights

**Internal program:** source document for a future post
**Prices checked:** 2026-08-30
**Price convention:** EUR; shipping, tax, and import costs excluded

## How to use this program

Work through the 12 core flights in sequence: they establish broad coordinates, then revisit them through closer comparisons. The advanced flights are optional electives, not a second required course. Every named tea is a suggestion rather than a mandatory purchase; when stock changes, follow the substitution rule and preserve the lesson before the label. The basket reuses nine anchors across multiple core flights and includes nine single-use counterparts, so buy each selected offer only once. Essential, standard, and advanced paths trade cost against breadth or specificity; a higher tier is not a quality ranking. Prices and availability are current only on the checked date, and checkout costs may change the practical choice. This guide says what to compare and notice, but deliberately gives no brewing, water, teaware, or tasting-session procedure.

## Part I — Learn the coordinates

### Flight 1: Youth and transformation

Compare current, middle, and late-semi-aged productions of Dayi 7542 to build an age map while testing how much continuity a recipe name actually provides. The lesson is transformation with age as one influence among several, not a claim that age alone caused every difference.

#### Why this flight

7542 is a widely used Menghai/Dayi benchmark, and these sample-scale anchors return in later recipe, factory, storage, and synthesis flights.

#### Suggested teas

- [2025 DaYi 7542 batch 2501](https://kingteamall.com/products/2025-dayi-7542-cake-357g-puerh-sheng-cha-raw-tea) — King Tea Mall, 25 g, €7.72 (€3.09/10 g); storage unknown.
- [2015 DaYi 7542 first batch](https://kingteamall.com/products/2015-dayi-7542-cake-357g-puerh-sheng-cha-raw-tea) — King Tea Mall, 30 g, €7.72 (€2.57/10 g); vendor-stated dry, clean Guangzhou storage.
- [2007 DaYi 7542 batch 701](https://kingteamall.com/products/2007-dayi-7542-cake-357g-puerh-sheng-cha-raw-tea-batch-701) — King Tea Mall, 25 g, €14.59 (€5.84/10 g); vendor-stated natural Guangzhou storage with moderate humidity and temperature.

#### Why these teas

The 2025 batch is the current factory endpoint; the first-batch 2015 sample adds a middle semi-aged point with dry-stated storage; and the 2007 batch extends the line to late semi-age under differently stated natural storage. One vendor, recipe, producer, and small portions make the set practical, while the different batches keep the comparison honest.

#### Pay attention to

Color; fresh versus tertiary aroma; bitterness onset; astringency; integration; returning sweetness; and the length and character of the finish.

#### Limitations

Recipe name does not hold leaf material or manufacturing constant across eras. Year, batch, processing, compression, sampling history, and storage all vary; every identity and storage detail here is a vendor claim, and measured conditions and custody are unavailable.

#### Alternatives

Keep Menghai/Dayi 7542 and retain one current production plus one 10–20-year example. Relax batch and exact year before changing recipe or factory, and disclose every storage difference; add a third point only when it clarifies the timeline.

#### Budget

Essential and standard: €30.03 incremental for all three anchors. Advanced: €22.31 incremental for the 2025 and 2007 anchors; it omits the 2015 point because the broader storage sampler enters Flight 2.

### Flight 2: Storage changes the clock

Contrast the dry-stated 2015 and natural-Guangzhou 2007 anchors for a low-cost first orientation, then use the six-tea sampler to widen the map across Kunming, Banna, and Guangdong dry storage. The lesson is how storage labels can frame transformation, not that these unmatched teas isolate climate.

#### Why this flight

Storage can make teas of similar practical age present very differently. Learning its vocabulary early prevents age, region, or recipe from absorbing differences that may instead reflect storage history.

#### Suggested teas

- [2015 DaYi 7542 first batch](https://kingteamall.com/products/2015-dayi-7542-cake-357g-puerh-sheng-cha-raw-tea) — King Tea Mall, 30 g, €7.72 (€2.57/10 g); vendor-stated dry, clean Guangzhou storage.
- [2007 DaYi 7542 batch 701](https://kingteamall.com/products/2007-dayi-7542-cake-357g-puerh-sheng-cha-raw-tea-batch-701) — King Tea Mall, 25 g, €14.59 (€5.84/10 g); vendor-stated natural Guangzhou storage with moderate humidity and temperature.
- [Aged Raw Pu-erh Tea Storage Comparison Sampler, mixed 2000–2007](https://yunnansourcing.com/products/aged-raw-pu-erh-tea-storage-comparison-sampler) — Yunnan Sourcing, six 25 g portions (150 g), €62.27 (€4.15/10 g); two Kunming, two Banna, and two Guangdong dry-storage examples.

#### Why these teas

The two 7542s reuse Flight 1 purchases and provide contrasting seller vocabulary within one recipe. The sampler is special because it places two examples in each of three stated storage groups, giving standard and advanced paths a broader orientation than a single binary.

#### Pay attention to

Retained youth; liquor color; sharpness versus softened structure; storage aroma; integration; and thickness.

#### Limitations

“Dry” Guangzhou is not automatically documented cool/dry storage, and a city name is not a climate record. The sampler changes production, year, origin, and storage together; conditions, airflow, compression, custody, and continuity are not measured. This is deliberately broad orientation, not storage causation.

#### Alternatives

Require explicit cool/dry and warm/humid wording, then match age band, factory, recipe, compression, and portion as closely as stock allows. If production identity differs, keep the flight framed as broad orientation.

#### Budget

Essential: €0 incremental, reusing the 2015 and 2007 Flight 1 anchors. Standard: €62.27 incremental for the sampler. Advanced: €62.27 incremental for the sampler, with the 2007 anchor reused.

### Flight 3: Factory and boutique

Compare a current numbered Dayi factory blend with Yunnan Sourcing's disclosed seven-component Menghai blend. The shared year, 25 g format, and close minimum prices make production model and disclosure easy to discuss without turning the flight into a vendor or quality ranking.

#### Why this flight

Factory recipe continuity and smaller Western-facing production are two influential ways current sheng reaches drinkers. Their different scale, audience, blend construction, and intended use shape expectations that price alone cannot explain.

#### Suggested teas

- [2025 DaYi 7542 batch 2501](https://kingteamall.com/products/2025-dayi-7542-cake-357g-puerh-sheng-cha-raw-tea) — King Tea Mall, 25 g, €7.72 (€3.09/10 g); numbered Menghai Tea Factory/Dayi blend, storage unknown.
- [2025 Yunnan Sourcing Cha Qi](https://yunnansourcing.com/products/2025-yunnan-sourcing-cha-qi-raw-pu-erh-tea-cake) — Yunnan Sourcing, 25 g, €7.21 (€2.88/10 g); vendor-stated blend of seven spring Menghai components.

#### Why these teas

The Dayi sample is a current production of the best-known factory benchmark used in Flight 1. Cha Qi is special for its unusually explicit seven-component construction and age-oriented stone-pressed framing, and it will return for the blend and construction lessons.

#### Pay attention to

Compression; smoke; aromatic clarity; structure; immediate accessibility; returning sweetness; finish; and durability.

#### Limitations

Factory versus boutique also changes producer, blend, material, processing, compression, and possibly storage. Dayi storage is unknown, and both identities and construction descriptions are vendor claims; the cups cannot isolate scale or establish relative quality.

#### Alternatives

Preserve a shared recent year, sample format, similar minimum price, and a clear large-factory versus disclosed small-brand construction contrast. Replace both sides if changing only one would create a different price or age band.

#### Budget

All tiers: €7.21 incremental for Cha Qi; the 2025 Dayi anchor is reused from Flight 1. The complete pair represents €14.93 of purchased tea.

### Flight 4: Yiwu and Bulang

Compare a vendor-stated Yiwu/Mengla tea with a Bulang/Menghai blend from the same seller, producer label, harvest year, sample size, and nearby price tier. Use the familiar softer/sweeter Yiwu and punchier/bitter Bulang tendencies as practitioner hypotheses to test, never as terroir laws.

#### Why this flight

Eastern and western Xishuangbanna form a useful first regional coordinate. A closely sourced pair makes those broad expectations legible while teaching that regional names contain substantial internal variation.

#### Suggested teas

- [2025 Yunnan Sourcing Wan Gong Village Wild Arbor](https://yunnansourcing.com/products/2025-yunnan-sourcing-wan-gong-village-wild-arbor-raw-pu-erh-tea-cake) — Yunnan Sourcing, 25 g, €8.33 (€3.33/10 g); vendor-stated April first flush from Wan Gong, Yiwu/Mengla, with wild-arbor-labelled material.
- [2025 Yunnan Sourcing Xin Ban'e](https://yunnansourcing.com/products/2025-yunnan-sourcing-xin-bane-raw-pu-erh-tea-cake) — Yunnan Sourcing, 25 g, €9.79 (€3.92/10 g); vendor-stated Lao Man'e and Xin Banzhang spring blend from Bulang/Menghai.

#### Why these teas

Wan Gong is a sample-scale eastern-Xishuangbanna anchor whose page states hand processing and low-temperature finishing. Xin Ban'e is a similarly priced western-Xishuangbanna counterpart explicitly presented as a potent bitter blend. Both can be reused in advanced regional work.

#### Pay attention to

Aromatic lift; sweetness; softness; bitterness; returning sweetness; coating texture; structure; and persistence.

#### Limitations

Origin, tree age, harvest, and sensory language are unverified vendor claims. The teas differ in village, material, blend status, and processing, and storage is unknown; neither one represents its whole region or proves a regional sensory rule.

#### Alternatives

Preserve one seller and producer label, year, season, sample size, and price tier while selecting explicitly named eastern-Xishuangbanna and Bulang/Menghai material. Keep micro-area and tree-age statements labeled as vendor claims.

#### Budget

All tiers: €18.12 incremental for both new anchors—€8.33 for Wan Gong and €9.79 for Xin Ban'e.

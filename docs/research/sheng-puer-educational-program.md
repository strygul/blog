# Learning sheng puer through comparative flights

## Purpose and evidence standard

This is a validity-first course in comparative tasting. Its twelve sensory flights ask narrow questions about selected teas; its three methods labs examine evidence and expectation questions that flavor cannot settle. “Foundation” and “advanced” describe learning order, not tea quality or price. A flight may show a repeatable difference in this set without proving a universal rule, authenticating a label, or isolating a single cause.

Use these evidence labels in notes and discussion:

- **Observed** — something recorded directly in a session or visibly documented on a checked storefront, limited to what was actually seen.
- **Vendor claim** — an identity, origin, material, processing, storage, or sensory statement made by a seller and not independently verified.
- **Practitioner hypothesis** — a specialist's experience-based expectation worth testing, not a promised outcome or product fact.
- **Inference** — the participant's interpretation of observations in light of the documented comparison and its confounders.
- **Unknown** — information that the available record does not establish.

Offer identities, sizes, purchase states, and native prices were checked on 2026-09-01. Availability can change. Product names and page descriptions remain vendor claims where the underlying identity or history is not independently documented.

## How to run every sensory flight

Use 5 g of leaf in each identical neutral porcelain vessel with a practical brewing volume of 100 ml. Use one water source at 100 °C, give every tea one five-second rinse, then brew for 10, 10, 15, 20, 30, and 45 seconds. Continue after the sixth infusion when the teas remain expressive. Apply the same timing, pour speed, water volume, vessel preheating, and drinking order to every cup in the set.

Have another person randomize the teas under neutral codes, or prepare codes in advance so identity is hidden during note-taking. Record dry- and wet-leaf aroma, liquor aroma, bitterness, sweetness, astringency, texture, aftertaste, perceived development, and change across infusions. Commit the first observations and preference ranking while blind, then reveal the identities. Repeat the comparison with new codes on a different day. A principal contrast counts as useful only when it is reasonably repeatable; report an unstable, unclear, or contradictory result as **inconclusive**.

Practical pacing accommodations are welcome. If a tea needs a different ratio, temperature, timing, vessel, or other parameter, first finish the shared protocol. Changing a parameter for only one tea ends the controlled side-by-side phase; label all later notes as an exploratory follow-up rather than evidence from the comparison.

## Comparative program

### Flight 1: Development states within Dayi 7542

#### Question

How do three selected Dayi 7542 productions with different stated years, batches, and storage histories differ under one protocol?

#### Difficulty and path

**Foundation.** This introduces development as a multivariable observation before later recipe and factory comparisons.

#### Suggested teas

- [2025 DaYi 7542 batch 2501](https://kingteamall.com/products/2025-dayi-7542-cake-357g-puerh-sheng-cha-raw-tea) — King Tea Mall, 25 g, USD 8.99 / €7.75. The live offer identity and size are observed storefront facts; recipe and batch identity are vendor claims; storage is unknown.
- [2015 DaYi 7542 first batch](https://kingteamall.com/products/2015-dayi-7542-cake-357g-puerh-sheng-cha-raw-tea) — King Tea Mall, 30 g, USD 8.99 / €7.75. First-batch identity and dry, clean Guangzhou storage are vendor claims; measured conditions and custody are unknown.
- [2007 DaYi 7542 batch 701](https://kingteamall.com/products/2007-dayi-7542-cake-357g-puerh-sheng-cha-raw-tea-batch-701) — King Tea Mall, 25 g, USD 16.99 / €14.65. Batch identity and natural Guangzhou storage with moderate humidity and temperature are vendor claims.

#### Why this set

One vendor, producer, recipe label, and practical sample format make a coherent three-point development exercise. The set deliberately treats chronology, batch, manufacture, compression, and storage as intertwined rather than calling it an age experiment.

#### Hold constant

Use the shared protocol and equal 5 g portions. Break comparable pieces from similarly situated parts of each sample when possible, and keep code assignment and cup position randomized.

#### Observe

Record liquor color, aroma family, bitterness onset and duration, astringency location and persistence, texture, sweetness, aftertaste, and how each dimension changes across infusions.

#### Confounders

Year, batch, source material, processing, compression, storage, sampling history, and custody differ or are unknown. A recipe number is not a fixed bill of materials.

#### Allowed conclusion

How the three selected productions differ across stated development histories.

#### Cannot establish

An age-only effect or a universal 7542 aging trajectory.

#### Substitution rule

Keep Dayi 7542 and retain at least three meaningfully separated production years. Prefer one seller and documented batches; disclose storage gaps before relaxing seller or batch matching.

#### Cost and reuse

€30.15 for three unique purchases. The 2007 tea returns in Flight 6; all three can support documentation work in Lab B.

#### Question

### Lab A: Storage evidence audit

#### Question

What can four common forms of storage evidence support, and where does each stop?

#### Materials

Use Flight 2's matched commercial pair and page; the [Yunnan Sourcing aged raw storage sampler page](https://yunnansourcing.com/products/aged-raw-pu-erh-tea-storage-comparison-sampler); city or climate labels already attached to selected Dayi examples; and the two Yee On warehouse descriptions used in Flight 12. The sampler and Yee On teas are documentation, not required lab purchases.

#### Procedure

Extract only explicit page statements. Classify each as observed page wording, vendor claim, inference, or unknown. Then build an evidence-strength table before reading tasting notes.

#### Record

| Evidence form | Strongest supported use | Unsupported inference |
|---|---|---|
| F2 matched commercial pair | A plausible storage-associated difference in the named set | Measured climate effect or complete custody |
| Multi-tea storage sampler page | How a seller groups several labelled storage categories | A controlled city comparison across different teas |
| City or climate label | The seller's stated location or broad condition | Actual continuous temperature, humidity, cleanliness, or airflow |
| Yee On warehouse description | The seller's stated process for each complete stored tea | One isolated cellar-duration, airing, or intensity effect |

#### Supported conclusion

Storage evidence gains specificity when production matching and handling documentation improve, but every form retains explicit unknowns.

#### Cannot establish

The lab cannot reconstruct custody, validate a city-wide profile, or infer measured storage conditions from flavor.

#### Cost and reuse

No additional purchase. It reuses F2 and F12 pages and selected teas; the sampler is a reference page only.

### Lab B: Claims are not flavors

#### Question

#### Materials

Use selected pages and any already-owned teas. Useful reference pages include Yunnan Sourcing [Cha Qi](https://yunnansourcing.com/products/2025-yunnan-sourcing-cha-qi-raw-pu-erh-tea-cake) for disclosed blend construction, [Xi Niu Tang Pasha](https://yunnansourcing.com/products/2025-yunnan-sourcing-xi-niu-tang-pasha-raw-pu-erh-tea-cake) for a single-area claim, [Yi Bang](https://yunnansourcing.com/products/2025-yunnan-sourcing-yi-bang-wild-arbor-raw-pu-erh-tea-cake) for processing and compression claims, Farmer Leaf [Jingmai Miyun](https://www.farmer-leaf.com/products/spring-2026-jingmai-miyun) and [Jingmai Gulan](https://www.farmer-leaf.com/products/spring-2026-jingmai-gulan) for garden-history claims, and [White2Tea School Day](https://white2tea.com/products/2025-school-day) for audience and disclosure choices.

#### Procedure

Create rows for **production model**, **blend/single-area**, **tree age/garden**, **processing**, **compression**, **intended aging**, and **disclosure philosophy**. Copy the smallest relevant page statement, label it observed wording or vendor claim, list what is unknown, and only then compare any available cups blind.

#### Record

For each category, record the page, exact claim type, direct sensory observations, participant inference, and at least one alternative explanation. Keep missing disclosure as unknown rather than negative evidence.

#### Supported conclusion

How the selected documentation differs and how the participant's recorded experience relates to it.

#### Cannot establish

Flavor cannot authenticate production scale, origin, blend construction, tree age, garden history, processing intent, compression history, intended aging, or a vendor's philosophy.

#### Cost and reuse

No additional purchase. Pages are sufficient; cup comparison is optional and reuses selected or reference teas already on hand.

### Lab C: Blind value and expectation

#### Question

How does learning a tea's name and price change this participant's sensory score, preference, and estimated value?

#### Materials

- [2025 Yunnan Sourcing Bing Dao Lao Zhai](https://yunnansourcing.com/products/2025-yunnan-sourcing-bing-dao-lao-zhai-raw-pu-erh-tea-cake) — 10 g, USD 9.00 / €7.76. The sample offer is observed; origin, tree age, production size, and sensory claims are vendor claims.
- [2025 Yunnan Sourcing Mo Lie Shan](https://yunnansourcing.com/products/2025-yunnan-sourcing-mo-lie-shan-raw-pu-erh-tea-cake) — 25 g, USD 7.60 / €6.55. The offer is observed; nearby Mengku geography, origin, material, and sensory claims are vendor claims. This is a different 2025 production from Flight 9's 2024 Mo Lie.

#### Procedure

Have another person randomize the samples under neutral codes. Brew one blind round with the shared protocol, then record a sensory score, preference, and estimated value before revealing either identity. Reveal the names and prices, explain the unequal purchase sizes and per-gram source prices, then repeat the evaluation and record score changes. Repeat on another day if the first response is unstable.

#### Record

Record blind and informed sensory scores, preference order, estimated value, confidence, and the signed change for each measure. Keep sensory observations separate from value judgments.

#### Supported conclusion

Only the participant's expectation response to this name-and-price disclosure is measured.

#### Cannot establish

The lab cannot show that price, prestige, scarcity, authenticity, origin, or objective quality was tasted, nor generalize one participant's response to other people.

#### Cost and reuse

€14.31 for two purchases, both included in the complete basket. Lab C is the only methods lab that adds required tea.

## Minimal glossary

- **Sheng puer:** raw puer tea made from Yunnan large-leaf material and capable of changing through manufacture, storage, and time.
- **Young / semi-aged / older:** practical course labels: under 10 years, 10–20 years, and over 20 years. They are not scientific maturity grades.
- **Recipe number:** a factory naming convention such as 7542 or 8582. It is neither a full bill of materials nor proof that different productions are identical.
- **Batch:** a production run within a year; `801` conventionally denotes the first 2008 production, subject to documentation and naming exceptions.
- **Storage history:** the tea's handling conditions and chronology. A city label alone does not document temperature, humidity, airflow, cleanliness, or custody.
- **Traditional Hong Kong storage:** an intentionally warm, humid commercial cellar phase followed by drier rest or airing; exact treatment must come from the tea's documentation.
- **Returning sweetness:** sweetness or sweet sensation perceived after bitterness or swallowing; record timing and duration rather than assuming an origin.
- **Confounder:** a variable that differs alongside the intended focus and offers another plausible explanation for the observations.

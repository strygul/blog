# Tetsubin history series — image ledger

**Access date:** 2026-07-31

## Provenance rules

- Log every externally sourced image before embedding it. Store a local copy under the relevant post asset directory; do not hotlink.
- Preserve the original URL, creator or institution, object title, access date, stated rights, and the required credit line. Do not crop marks, accession numbers, or other identification evidence.
- Record conflicts or unclear rights in their own notes; a credit line does not itself grant reuse permission.
- Use only these rights or permission statuses: `public domain`, `CC0`, `licensed reuse`, `permission granted`, `credit requested / permission unconfirmed`, and `original SVG`.
- In `Notes`, `licensed reuse` must give the exact licence name/version and its URL; `permission granted` must give the dated permission evidence, rights holder, and scope of the grant. Do not use either status without that evidence.
- Prefer institutional, government/association, curator/research, and workshop images. Retailer and auction images are discovery or context only and must be labeled as such in the notes.

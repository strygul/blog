nor does it assume that one sample represents every tea carrying the same place name.

<h2>Why This Set</h2>

Using three samples reduces the temptation to treat a single contrast as a fixed regional axis. One tea may differ from
the other two, all three may separate, or substantial overlap may be the most important result.

The set is commercially coherent, but origin, tree age, leaf population, lot composition, processing, compression, and
storage remain unverified or incompletely matched.

<h2>Flight-Specific Controls</h2>

Use three vessels and hide the subregional names and prices. Rotate positions between infusions only if the movement can
be performed reliably; otherwise keep positions stable and assign new ones for the repeat.

<h2>What to Observe</h2>

For all three codes, record:

- leaf, liquor, and emptied-cup aroma;
- bitterness onset, location, intensity, and duration;
- sweetness and returning sweetness;
- astringency location and persistence;
- texture, apparent body, and salivation;
- aftertaste and cooling behavior;
- extraction curve, late-infusion persistence, and degree of overlap within the set.

Do not look for predetermined village markers. Describe similarities as carefully as differences.

<h2>What We Can Learn From It</h2>

This tasting shows us the differences and overlaps we found among these Yiwu- and Mengla-area labels from the same vendor and year.

This small group cannot give every place a fixed taste. We also cannot confirm village origin or tree age from the cup, and leaf material, lot, processing, compression, and storage may explain some of what we noticed.

To build a better picture, we would need many teas from each subregion, across different producers and years, with storage kept as similar as possible. Repeating the comparisons blind with other people would help us look for patterns without turning them into rules too quickly.

<h2>If One Sample Is Unavailable</h2>

Use at least three Yiwu/Mengla-labelled samples from one seller and producer label with the same year, season, portion,
and close price. Prefer explicit lot and processing documentation. If only two valid samples remain, postpone this
three-point flight rather than presenting a binary comparison under the same question.

<h2>Question</h2>

How do product-page claims and the participant's cup experience relate without turning claims into flavor facts?

<h2>Materials</h2>

Use selected pages and any already-owned teas. Useful reference pages include Yunnan Sourcing [Cha Qi](https://yunnansourcing.com/products/2025-yunnan-sourcing-cha-qi-raw-pu-erh-tea-cake) for disclosed blend construction, [Xi Niu Tang Pasha](https://yunnansourcing.com/products/2025-yunnan-sourcing-xi-niu-tang-pasha-raw-pu-erh-tea-cake) for a single-area claim, [Yi Bang](https://yunnansourcing.com/products/2025-yunnan-sourcing-yi-bang-wild-arbor-raw-pu-erh-tea-cake) for processing and compression claims, Farmer Leaf [Jingmai Miyun](https://www.farmer-leaf.com/products/spring-2026-jingmai-miyun) and [Jingmai Gulan](https://www.farmer-leaf.com/products/spring-2026-jingmai-gulan) for garden-history claims, and [White2Tea School Day](https://white2tea.com/products/2025-school-day) for audience and disclosure choices.

<h2>Procedure</h2>

Create rows for **production model**, **blend/single-area**, **tree age/garden**, **processing**, **compression**, **intended aging**, and **disclosure philosophy**. Copy the smallest relevant page statement, label it observed wording or vendor claim, list what is unknown, and only then compare any available cups blind.

<h2>Record</h2>

For each category, record the page, exact claim type, direct sensory observations, participant inference, and at least one alternative explanation. Keep missing disclosure as unknown rather than negative evidence.

<h2>Supported Conclusion</h2>

How the selected documentation differs and how the participant's recorded experience relates to it.

<h2>Cannot Establish</h2>

Flavor cannot authenticate production scale, origin, blend construction, tree age, garden history, processing intent, compression history, intended aging, or a vendor's philosophy.

<h2>Cost and Reuse</h2>

No additional purchase. Pages are sufficient; cup comparison is optional and reuses selected or reference teas already on hand.

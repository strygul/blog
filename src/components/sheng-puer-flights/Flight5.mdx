<h2>What This Flight Asks</h2>

How do the selected 2008 Dayi 7542 and 8582 samples differ under one blind, repeatable protocol?

Recipe numbers are useful identifiers, but they are not complete bills of materials or quality scores. This pair is an
orientation to two commercial samples, not an experiment that isolates recipe construction.

The two samples cost **€23.27**, excluding shipping, tax, currency conversion, and import costs. The live 25 g variants
were observable storefront facts. For the 7542, the page title omits a batch, the URL says 903, and a separately named
batch-801 cake was unavailable; the selected sample therefore does not establish a batch. Its Guangzhou dry/clean
storage is vendor wording. The 8582 recipe, batch 801, and Guangzhou natural storage are also vendor claims.

Do not silently normalize the 7542 to either batch 801 or 903. That ambiguity is part of the evidence record.

<h2>Why This Set</h2>

Producer, seller, year, 25 g portion size, and broad storage city are close. Both samples can also be reused in Flight
11, making the purchase efficient. The remaining batch, material, processing, compression, and storage-wording
differences are substantial enough that a causal recipe claim would be too strong.

The purpose is to learn how these two samples present relative to each other and to practise keeping an imperfect
commercial match honest.

<h2>Flight-Specific Controls</h2>

Hide recipe numbers, batch wording, and prices. Do not use conventional recipe-number leaf-grade interpretations as a
cue for quality or expected flavor, and do not compensate for one cup by changing only its next infusion.

<h2>What to Observe</h2>

Record the same dimensions for both codes:

- leaf, liquor, and emptied-cup aroma;
- bitterness onset, intensity, and duration;
- sweetness and returning sweetness;
- astringency location and persistence;
- texture, apparent body, and liquor weight;
- aftertaste and cooling behavior;
- the extraction pattern and direction of change through the sequence.

<h2>What the Result Can Support</h2>

The allowed conclusion is: **this is how the two selected same-year Dayi recipe samples differed under this protocol**.

The flight cannot establish recipe alone as the cause, a universal 7542 or 8582 profile, a quality hierarchy, or the
7542 batch. Storage wording, batch, material, processing, compression, sampling, and custody remain unmatched or
unknown.

<h2>If One Sample Is Unavailable</h2>

Require Dayi 7542 and 8582 from the same year and, preferably, the same documented batch, seller, sample format, and
storage history. Preserve explicit uncertainty rather than guessing a batch. Do not substitute a different year simply
to complete the pair.

Both teas return in Flight 11, where a selected 7532 adds a third recipe coordinate.

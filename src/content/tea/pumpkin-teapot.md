I have little to no affection for Yixing pots from the mid-80s through the 90s…

In general, I find them much worse in quality than pots from the periods before and after — with a huge number of 
counterfeits flooding the market and all sorts of additives and chemicals making their way into the clay.

And yet, I have this lovely half-handmade pumpkin from the 90s.

For no particular reason other than that I find it ridiculously cute.

## What This Flight Asks

How do selected Mengku-side and Bangdong-side 2024 Lincang examples differ under the same blind protocol?

The exercise keeps seller, producer label, year, April harvest framing, prefecture, and sample size close. It does not
test a fixed east–west signature or authenticate either stated origin.

## Why This Set

The commercial match is close enough to make a focused within-prefecture comparison practical. Retaining the page
inconsistency also makes the flight a useful lesson in not converting loose geographical proximity into provenance.

The sensory task is independent of that uncertainty: the cups can differ repeatably even when the labels do not support
a precise regional cause.

## Set Up the Flight

Use two identical neutral porcelain vessels, two matching cups, a scale, timer, one kettle, and one water source.

1. Weigh **5 g of each tea** into vessels with a practical brewing volume of **100 ml**.
2. Select comparably intact fragments and note visible differences.
3. Assign neutral codes A and B. Keep maps, village pages, prices, and expected profiles hidden.
4. Preheat the vessels equally and use water at **100 °C**.
5. Give each tea a five-second rinse, then brew for **10, 10, 15, 20, 30, and 45 seconds**.
6. Keep water volume, pour speed, cooling time, and drinking order consistent. Use a fixed pouring sequence and mark
   mistimed infusions.

Randomize positions for both sessions. Review the maps and documentation only after the first blind descriptions and
preference ranking are committed.

## What to Observe

Record for each code:

- leaf, liquor, and emptied-cup aroma;
- bitterness onset, intensity, and duration;
- sweetness and returning sweetness;
- astringency location and persistence;
- texture, body, and salivation;
- finish and cooling behavior;
- extraction curve and change through later infusions.

Do not search for a predetermined Mengku- or Bangdong-side marker. The comparison is successful if it produces an
honest, repeatable description—even if the samples substantially overlap.

## Repeat Before Drawing a Conclusion

Repeat the complete flight on a different day with new codes and positions. Hold water, vessels, 5 g / 100 ml ratio,
temperature, rinse, infusion schedule, cooling time, and pouring sequence constant.

If the principal contrast is unstable, contradictory, or unclear across the two sessions, report the flight as
**inconclusive**.

## What the Result Can Support

The allowed conclusion is: **this is how the selected Mengku- and Bangdong-side examples differed under this protocol,
subject to the documented geographic uncertainty**.

The flight cannot establish a general sensory boundary inside Lincang, resolve the Na Han page inconsistency, verify
origin or tree age, or isolate geography from the other unmatched variables.

## If One Sample Is Unavailable

Keep one Mengku-side and one Bangdong-side Lincang example under one seller and producer label with matched year,
harvest framing, portion size, and similar price. Preserve geographical ambiguity explicitly instead of promoting a
nearby name into verified origin.

## What This Flight Asks

How do the two portions in one seller-described 2007 XiZi Hao Shangpin storage set differ under the same protocol?

Both portions are sold together under one production name, with one labelled **Taiwan humid storage** and the other
**Hou De USA storage**. This reduces the material and manufacturing differences that would be introduced by comparing
unrelated teas. It remains a commercial match based on seller documentation, not a controlled storage experiment.

The single 32 g set cost **USD 15.00 / €12.94**, excluding shipping, tax, currency conversion, and import costs. The
visible listing, 16 g + 16 g split, price, and add-to-cart state were observable marketplace facts when checked. The
production identity and both storage histories are vendor claims.

The listing does not establish exact batch or cake identity, the portions' initial condition, storage dates, measured
temperature and humidity, airflow, packaging, custody, or the USA storage protocol. Preserve those unknowns in the
notes. Each portion supplies three 5 g controlled sessions, which is enough for the required first tasting and repeat.

## Why This Set

Storage comparisons are often weakened by using different productions. Here, the seller presents the samples as two
portions of the same named year, producer, and production. That makes a storage-associated interpretation more
plausible if a contrast repeats, while still leaving the documentation and handling gaps visible.

The goal is not to identify a “Taiwan taste” or a “USA taste.” It is to determine whether these two particular portions
can be distinguished in a stable way and to describe the difference without assigning more certainty than the match
supports.

## Set Up the Flight

Use two identical neutral porcelain vessels, two matching cups, a scale, timer, one kettle, and the same water for the
whole session.

1. Weigh **5 g from each 16 g portion** into vessels with a practical brewing volume of **100 ml**.
2. Select equally intact leaf where possible. Do not compare a compact chunk from one portion with only dust and loose
   fragments from the other.
3. Do not air, condition, or rest only one portion before the comparison.
4. Have another person assign neutral codes A and B. If tasting alone, conceal the labels before setting out the cups.
5. Use water at **100 °C**, give each tea a five-second rinse, then brew for **10, 10, 15, 20, 30, and 45 seconds**.
6. Keep water volume, vessel preheating, pour speed, cooling time, and drinking order consistent. Use a fixed pouring
   sequence and mark any obvious timing error.

Randomize cup position for the first session and use new positions and codes for the repeat. Do not add an extra rinse
to the portion you expect to have received more humid storage during the controlled phase.

## What to Observe

Record the following without guessing the storage label:

- dry-leaf, warmed-leaf, liquor, and wet-leaf aroma;
- liquor color and clarity;
- bitterness onset, intensity, and duration;
- astringency location and persistence;
- texture and apparent body;
- sweetness, returning sweetness, and finish;
- perceived development and change across infusions;
- whether the main contrast remains stable as the cups cool.

## Repeat Before Drawing a Conclusion

Repeat the complete flight on another day with new codes and cup positions. Keep the water, vessels, leaf ratio,
temperature, rinse, infusion schedule, cooling time, and pouring sequence unchanged.

A useful result requires the principal contrast to recur. The wording need not be identical, but its direction and
location in the infusion sequence should be recognizably stable. If it disappears, reverses, or remains unclear, report
the flight as **inconclusive**.

## What the Result Can Support

The allowed conclusion is: **a plausible storage-associated difference was—or was not—repeatably observed within this
seller's matched commercial set**.

The flight cannot establish measured humidity or temperature effects, exact custody, a universal Taiwan or USA storage
profile, or storage as the only cause. Flavor also cannot authenticate the production or either storage label.

## If the Set Is Unavailable

Require two portions of the same documented production with distinct seller-described storage histories. Matching the
year alone is not enough. If production identity cannot be held, use the pages as material for the storage-evidence lab
rather than presenting the teas as a replacement for this flight.

The page and both portions can be reused in Lab A. No additional storage sampler purchase is required.
